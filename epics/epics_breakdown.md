# Epics System Specification (`epics/`)

- **Epic 1: Customer Management**
  - Registration, JWT Login, User Profile, Address Manager, Customer Table.
- **Epic 2: Product & Inventory Management**
  - Product CRUD, Inventory Stock Control, Low Stock Badging.
- **Epic 3: Online Shopping, Checkout & Orders**
  - Product Catalog, Cart Drawer, 3-Step Checkout, Coupon Engine (`STYLE25`), Order Tracking Timeline, Printable Invoice Generator.
- **Epic 4: Admin Dashboard, Reports & AI Stylist**
  - Executive Analytics Dashboard, Employee & Staff Directory, Promo Coupon Engine, AI Recommendation Stylist Widget.
